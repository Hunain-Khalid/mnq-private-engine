# Experiment Log: v0.2.02 (Dual-Leg Sprinter & ATR Trailing Stop Audit)

## 🎯 Research Hypothesis & Objective
- **Phase:** In-Sample Training (IS) [Execution Timing & Short Leak Audit]
- **Target Asset:** SP_500_E_MINI (ES) / MNQ
- **Thesis:** Deploying a dual-leg execution model (Sprinter scalp + Lagger trend rider) with a 9:31 AM EST market-open schedule will increase trade frequency and capture macro trends cleanly.
- **Economic Rationale:** Splitting volume into a fast profit-taking leg and a trailing momentum leg isolates high-frequency noise from macro drift, optimizing Alpha Velocity and Payoff Asymmetry.

## 🏛️ Architectural Rules
1. **9:31 AM Opening Range Entry:** Firing entries immediately at cash open to capture opening volatility.
2. **Dual-Leg Sizing:** 50/50 contract split between Sprinter and Lagger.
3. **ATR Trailing Stop:** Lagger trailing dynamically behind price action via ATR multiple (2.5x).

## 🔍 Critical Diagnostics Findings
- **The Execution Timing Bug:** Discovered that the scheduler fired at 10:00 AM EST instead of 9:31 AM, missing opening range momentum.
- **The MFE Giveback Problem:** Profit Capture dropped to `-18.2% of MFE`, proving that the Sprinter and Lagger were fighting each other and giving back all floating profits before exit. 
- **Overstay Drag:** Dead trade time cost $22,475 in accumulated losses.
