# Experiment Log: v0.2.02 (Multi-Leg Runner/Lagger & Timing Diagnostic)

## 🎯 Research Hypothesis & Objective
- **Thesis:** Implementing a dual-leg execution model (1 Sprinter for quick scalps, 2 Laggers for trend riding) combined with single HMA(3) slope direction would increase trade frequency and capture macro trends.
- **Test Parameters:** In-Sample (IS) training run from 2012-01-01 to 2019-06-30 on ES futures. 

## 📊 Empirical Results & Performance Metrics
- **Sharpe Ratio:** -1.07 (Failed / Severe Drawdown)
- **Net P&L:** -$124,900.00 (-12.5% drawdown on $1M capital)
- **Total Trades:** 3,338 (Massive increase in execution frequency as hypothesized)
- **Long Cohort P&L:** -$5,987.50 (Win Rate: 53.1%)
- **Short Cohort P&L:** -$118,912.50 (Win Rate: 40.5% | Avg Loss: -$257.31)
- **Max Drawdown:** 15.0% (-$130,088)

## 🔍 Quantitative Findings & Failure Analysis
1. **The Short-Side Asymmetry Catastrophe:** The short cohort was an absolute capital leak (`-$118k`), proving that a simple trailing HMA slope fails catastrophically when shorting a secular 7-year equity bull market (2012–2019) without a macro regime filter.
2. **Execution Timing Bug (The 10:00 AM Entry Leak):** The algorithm was scheduled to fire at `after_market_open(..., 30)`, causing entries to execute at **10:00 AM EST** instead of the cash session open at **9:30 AM EST**. This meant missing the high-alpha opening range breakout window entirely.
3. **Duration & EOD Mismatch:** Average trade duration was ~6.5 hours, colliding head-on with the 3:45 PM EOD flat rule. Positions were regularly forced closed at the worst possible micro-structure moments.

## 🚀 Next Engineering Iteration (v0.2.03)
- **Fix Entry Timing:** Shift schedule rule to enter at **9:31 AM EST** to capture the opening range.
- **Short-Side Gating:** Temporarily disable short entries or enforce strict ATR-channel volatility gating before allowing short positions during bull regimes.
- **Duration Tuning:** Shorten Sprinter target to 15 points and let Lagger ride with an ATR-trailing stop rather than relying solely on EOD flat liquidation.
